Spacetime, the stars, and what is known about them

The Night Sky

A place to build accurate pictures of how the universe works, and to use them until they are habits. Everything here is standard physics, checked; where something is still argued over, it says so.

Each step has a chamber to play, which ends with the proper terms, the math underneath for when you want it (the calculus is rebuilt a piece at a time, where an idea first needs it), checked links to go deeper, and practice without end. Nineteen steps in seven stages; six are built. Stages A to C run in order. After them, D and E make one line and F another, and G needs both.
