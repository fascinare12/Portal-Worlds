A playable companion to Gödel, Escher, Bach

The Spire

Douglas Hofstadter's book builds one idea out of many small ones: that meaning, and in the end a self, can come out of symbols that only follow rules. Reading it gives you the ideas. This is a place to use them until they are yours.

Each step has a chamber, to be played before or beside its chapter, which ends with a card tying what you did to the book's own words. Each also has practice: short puzzles, as many as you like, that ask for the same move in different clothes.

In the book

Hofstadter opens with this canon as his first Strange Loop: you move up through the levels of a system and find yourself back where you started. He calls a system in which that happens a Tangled Hierarchy. The whole book is an argument that minds are loops of this kind.
