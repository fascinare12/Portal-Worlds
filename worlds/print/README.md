# The Printer's World

The world behind the parchment-coloured pool in the wood. **Not built yet**: for now
the pool leads to a bare sheet of cream paper with only the outline block
printed, and a button home.

## The idea

A world that is being woodblock-printed as you move through it. Ahead of you
it is still assembling, one block at a time: black outlines first, then a pale
indigo wash, then Prussian blue, then the last few colours. Behind you it is a
finished print. The paper is cream washi with visible grain. The mountain is
on every horizon, whichever way you turn. Fishing villages, arched bridges and
small figures caught mid-action, all flat.

## The pool

Looking into the pool in the wood shows the same idea in small: the pool's
own surface is the sheet, and on it a print of the mountain, the sea and a boat is made block by
block, admired, lifted away and begun again. That picture is drawn in
`src/world/pools.ts` (`printPicture`).
