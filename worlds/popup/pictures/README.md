# Painted pictures

Files here take the place of the cut-paper drawings in `../art.ts`.

- `<name>.png`: a picture, with a clear background (`pig.png`, `wolf.png`).
- `<name>.jpg`: a paper, filling the whole square (`straw.jpg`).
- `page-<story>-<number>.jpg`: the background of one spread of a story, as
  wide to tall as the open book, 26 to 19 (`page-pigs-1.jpg`).

The names are the ones in `../art.ts` and the story ids in `../story.ts`.
Anything without a file here keeps its drawing. Nothing else has to change.

`scripts/illustrate.mjs` makes them with Replicate; see the top of that file.
