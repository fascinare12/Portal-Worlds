# The deep sea

The world behind the ice-blue pool in the wood. There is nothing to win and
nothing to read: it is a place to sink through and touch.

## Moving

- Drag up, scroll or pinch to sink; the reverse to rise. Arrow keys work too.
- Drag sideways to turn and look around.
- The bar on the right shows how deep you are. The green button goes home.

## Going down

1. **Sunlit shallows.** The rippling surface overhead, shafts of sunlight
   standing in the water, silver and gold shoals.
2. **The coral reef**, about thirty units down: brain corals, staghorn
   thickets, ridged cup corals, cauliflower and pillar corals, tube sponges,
   anemones and seagrass that wave, lacy sea fans and starfish, their surfaces
   taken from a photograph of pitted coral rock; and nine kinds of reef fish
   (clownfish, blue tang, butterflyfish, bannerfish, parrotfish, damselfish,
   sergeant majors, anthias and wrasse), each with its own shape and markings, all under the moving net
   of light the waves throw down. Pebbles and coral rubble lie on rippled
   sand, and a sea turtle paddles a slow circle overhead. The reef rings a "blue hole"; keep sinking
   and you pass through it.
3. **Twilight.** Deep blue. Two manta rays wheeling, comb jellies with rainbows running down them,
   lanternfish with rows of lights along their sides, and a whale and her calf that cross about once a minute.
4. **Midnight.** Black water lit only by living things: plankton, chains of
   light, anglerfish with glowing lures.
5. **The deep floor.** Pitted, cracked stone that shows by a faint light you
   carry with you, seams of glowing heat round the fire vents, hills threaded with veins of light, spires of veined
   rock, blunt open-mouthed vents, a few with fire in their throats and most just smoking, tube worms
   with red plumes, tall glowing sea pens, and gardens of glowing stalks.

## Touching

- **Tap open water:** a wave of light spreads from the spot through the
  plankton, and sets the veins in the seafloor blazing as it passes. Waves
  also set off by themselves every few seconds in the deep. Each wave takes
  the colour of the note it rang with: five notes, five colours.
- **Tap a jellyfish:** it flashes, rings and jets upward. Each jellyfish has
  its own note, big ones low. Notes ring for about six seconds and all come
  from one five-note scale, so tapping several builds a chord.
- **Tap an anglerfish:** its lure flares and shows the fish behind it.
- **Hold a finger in the water:** plankton lights along it, and fish near it
  bolt forward or wheel round and dart back.

## Sound

Everything is made in the browser. Two held notes sit under it all, a lighter
pair near the surface giving way to a pair two octaves lower in the deep.
Chimes and the whale's call are sent through echoes and a four-second wash,
so they hang in the water. Every few seconds the sea rings a note by itself:
a jellyfish near you lights up and sounds, or in the dark a wave of light
sets off with a low note. The ♪ button turns it all off.

All fish have fins, eyes, a darker back and paler belly, and bodies that flex
as they swim.
